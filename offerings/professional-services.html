Staff Augmentation

We provide skilled professionals to complement your in-house team on a temporary or project basis. This model enables quick ramp-up, cost control, and operational agility without the long-term commitment of full-time hiring.

  1. Fast onboarding of domain-relevant talent
  2. Scalable resources for short- or long-term needs
  3. Reduces overhead and hiring complexity


Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

Our RPO services streamline and manage your end-to-end recruitment function. We act as an extension of your HR team, driving efficiency, improving candidate quality, and reducing time-to-fill.

  1. Dedicated talent acquisition team
  2. Optimized hiring workflows and tools
  3. Scalable hiring aligned with business growth


Placement Services

AceSoft helps you find the right full-time hires with deep domain alignment. We manage the sourcing, screening, and evaluation processes to deliver high-quality, pre-vetted candidates.

  1. Full-time, permanent hiring solutions
  2. Tailored sourcing for niche and leadership roles
  3. Quality assurance through rigorous evaluation


Managed Time & Material (T&M)

We manage teams or resources under a time-based billing model with performance oversight. This model combines flexibility with delivery accountability.

  1. Transparent effort tracking and cost control
  2. Managed team performance with aligned KPIs
  3. Ideal for evolving project scopes


Shared Services

Our Shared Services model centralizes key support functions — from IT operations to HR and finance — delivering cost efficiencies, standardization, and process optimization.

  1. Centralized service hubs across functions
  2. Cost-effective and SLA-driven delivery
  3. Enhanced service consistency and compliance


Fixed Price Projects

For well-defined scopes and outcomes, we offer fixed-price execution with end-to-end responsibility. Ideal for milestone-driven or turnkey engagements.

  1. Clear deliverables, timeline, and budget
  2. Full accountability from planning to delivery
  3. Best for defined requirements and minimal changes


Payroll Services

AceSoft takes care of end-to-end payroll processing for your outsourced or in-house workforce — ensuring compliance, accuracy, and timely payments.

  1. Statutory compliance and tax handling
  2. Payslip generation, disbursals, and reporting
  3. Confidential, secure payroll processing


Hire, Train & Deploy (HTD)

We source fresh or junior talent, train them as per your project needs, and deploy them for immediate contribution. This model ensures skill readiness and cultural alignment.

  1. Customized training based on client tech stack
  2. Pre-assessed candidates aligned to roles
  3. Reduces time-to-productivity and onboarding gaps
